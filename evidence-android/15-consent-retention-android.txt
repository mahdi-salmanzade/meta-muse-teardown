======================================================================
6. NOTABLE TENSIONS VISIBLE IN TEXT/CODE
======================================================================

  - Calendar and Contacts consent says existing data is stored (HISTORICAL_SYNC); Messages, Call log and
    Health say "shared after you connect", yet SMS, call log and health data sources declare
    supportsBackfill = true with 30-day chunks and an unbounded item limit.
  - Every local connector footer says the connector's info "may" be used "to improve AI at Meta",
    including Health Connect data; custom connectors say "which we use" (R 0x7f1205a7).
  - Deleting a message does not remove it from memory (R 0x7f120592); disconnecting does not remove
    earlier data (R 0x7f1206c4).
  - Both training defaults found in code are true.
