VERDICT: caller checks found (signature allow-list). The inspected paths reject
untrusted identities rather than exposing the ID solely because they are exported.

Muse certificate was independently attributed, and PendingIntent delegation remains
a separate trust question (see uncertainties below).

## What remains uncertain
- Which Meta apps own each of the 10 non-Muse hashes. They are presumably Meta keys, but this was not verified.
- Whether any allow-listed Meta app re-exports the ID without a check. That is outside this APK.
- X/C19297iJ.A00 is marked "decompiled incorrectly". The explicit "is not trusted" throw
  and the provider's catch-all make the outcome clear. The exact flag-bit branches (j & 1/16/32/64/128) were
  not verified against smali.
- Residual theoretical risk: the receiver trusts the PendingIntent creator. A
  PendingIntent created by an allow-listed Meta app that leaked to a third party would
  pass the check. No such leak was found here.
