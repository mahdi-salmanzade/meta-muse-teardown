08 / the verdict
It asks permission.
Then it keeps the tap open.
Muse doesn't hack anything. It asks for broad access, and once you say yes, it has the code to keep syncing your life to a Meta VM, where training is on by default and deletion isn't guaranteed.
09 / if you installed it
Close the tap.
  1. Turn off every “Keep ___ up to date” switch and Photos sync
  2. Turn off AI training in Muse settings
  3. Revoke Full Disk Access, Accessibility, Screen Recording
  4. Remove “Muse Browser Node” from chrome://extensions
  5. Delete your cloud data — uninstalling doesn't
