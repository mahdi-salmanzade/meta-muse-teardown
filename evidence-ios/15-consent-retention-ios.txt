6. NOTABLE TENSIONS VISIBLE IN TEXT
======================================================================

  - The iOS consent footer says connector info is used ("which we use to improve AI at Meta"), where the
    Android local-connector footers say "which we may use".
  - "Pause" in the photo UI and the agent's "stop the sync" both leave automatic syncing on; syncing
    resumes at next app open.
  - Forwarded iMessages and camera-roll uploads persist after turning forwarding/sync off
    ("Messages already shared are not deleted"; "Previously uploaded messages are not deleted").
