FairPlay-decrypted App Store package, but do NOT authenticate the decrypted code.
`codesign --verify --deep --strict` fails. A matching header and matching pages outside
the encrypted ranges cannot exclude changes within the decrypted ranges. "No evidence
of modification" must not be read as "proved unmodified." The original page-hash
inspection below is retained for completeness; its verifier was not preserved here.
